Bots

A bot is an account that posts by itself, approved by the site's admins. A site bot runs on free feeds (RSS, Atom or JSON feeds from news sites, YouTube channels, Bluesky, Mastodon, Reddit, Substack and many more): anyone can propose one, and once approved it is the site's, run by the admins and linked to nobody. A member bot needs its member to run it, with a key for their own script (for example for X) or a paid source: it stays theirs and says who runs it. Bots post and forward; they never comment, vote, chat or take tips. How bots work
