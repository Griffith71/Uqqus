{% filter markdown %} # {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} Rules and Guidelines ## 1. Free Expression and Good Faith Good-faith discussion, disagreement and criticism are permitted. Deliberate deception and bad-faith conduct intended to mislead or harm others are prohibited. Claims of deliberate deception need clear, documented evidence: disagreement, unpopular opinions or speculation about motives are not enough on their own. ## 2. Criminal Activity and Prohibited Conduct Do not use {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} to engage in, facilitate or promote criminal activity or conduct prohibited by these rules. This includes harassment, scams (including gambling schemes), fraud, spam, unsolicited commercial promotion, violence, malware, phishing, cyberattacks and other unlawful activity. Do not infringe copyright or other intellectual property rights. Do not copy, repost or redistribute protected content without permission or a valid legal basis, or link to pirated copies. Do not falsely claim authorship of another person's work. Do not use deceptive impersonation of other users, public figures, organisations or {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} staff, and do not publish personal information, yours or anyone else's. ## 3. Content Standards Do not post content made mainly to be consumed as sexual, violent or grotesque material. A broader work is not prohibited merely because it contains such elements. Mark Sensitive content Sensitive. ## 4. Transparency and Disclosure Disclose commercial promotion, including paid partnerships and other material commercial relationships. Identify content created using AI. Automated accounts must clearly identify themselves as bots. **What is Sensitive?** Mark a post Sensitive if a film or game with the same content would be rated 15, R or 16+, or given a content warning. That means strong sexual content or nudity, graphic violence or injury, strong drug use, self-harm or suicide, or very distressing or frightening scenes. If you're not sure, mark it Sensitive. Sensitive posts and strong language are hidden from locked accounts, and from anyone in a country where the law requires it until they confirm their age. A locked account can't post at all until its age is confirmed. ## 5. Evading and Manipulating the Site Do not evade a {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} ban or suspension, or use another account to circumvent a block imposed by another user. No hacking, no trying to get into accounts or systems that are not yours, and no exploiting a flaw for advantage: if you find a problem, tell us. No manipulating votes, and no fake or coordinated accounts that give a false impression of support or activity. Do not try to find out who is behind an anonymous post or comment, and do not say who you think wrote one. Testing guesses, or comparing accounts, to unmask someone against their choice is unfair exposure and can lead to a ban. > Anonymous posts and comments follow the same rules as everything else. Anonymity hides who wrote them from other users, not from {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }}.

6. Bots

A bot is an account that posts by itself. Every bot follows all of the rules above, and in addition: * A bot account must clearly identify itself as a bot. Its profile says who runs it. * Every bot is proposed in Settings > Bots and approved by an admin before it posts anything. * Bots may post, forward, and help with moderation. Bots may not vote or comment. * A bot that posts must serve a useful function, such as looking up information or bridging to another service. * A bot that uses the API must send a unique `User-Agent` header and an `X-User-Type: Bot` header, must authenticate with an OAuth2 token, and should use the `/api/v1` endpoints whenever possible. How to set up a bot, and the limits that apply to it, are on the [Bots](/help/bots) page. {% endfilter %}
