6. Bots

A bot is an account that posts by itself. Every bot follows all of the rules above, and in addition: * A bot account must clearly identify itself as a bot. Its profile says who runs it. * Every bot is proposed in Settings > Bots and approved by an admin before it posts anything. * Bots may post, forward, and help with moderation. Bots may not vote or comment. * A bot that posts must serve a useful function, such as looking up information or bridging to another service. * A bot that uses the API must send a unique `User-Agent` header and an `X-User-Type: Bot` header, must authenticate with an OAuth2 token, and should use the `/api/v1` endpoints whenever possible. How to set up a bot, and the limits that apply to it, are on the [Bots](/help/bots) page. {% endfilter %} {% endblock %}
