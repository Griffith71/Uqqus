Search tips

Type words to find posts with them in the title or the text. Add operators to narrow the search, in any order:

cats "black cat" -dogs from:@alice in:+pets since:2026-01-01 has:image lang:en

Start a search with + to find guilds (+pets) or with @ to find accounts (@alice).

Words

  • black cat: posts with both words, anywhere in the title or the text.
  • "black cat": posts with those words together, in that order.
  • -dogs or -"hot dogs": leaves out posts with that word or those words.

Good to know

  • Days are in UTC. before: and after: leave the day itself out; since: and until: include it.
  • from: never finds an account's anonymous posts, and -from: never hides them. Nobody can find out who wrote an anonymous post by searching.
  • A search only finds what you could see anyway: your word filter, blocks, mutes and private guilds all still apply. A search for one account (from:) shows their posts even if you muted them.
  • An account or guild you can't see reads as one that does not exist.
  • A search can hold up to {{ search.MAX_PARTS }} words and operators, and up to {{ search.MAX_CHARS }} characters.
  • The search page has an Advanced search form that writes the operators for you.

Examples

  • from:@alice has:poll: Alice's polls.
  • in:+news domain:example.com since:2026-01-01: links to example.com in +news this year.
  • recipe lang:es -is:forward: recipes in Spanish, each post once.
  • "open source" min_votes:50 min_comments:10: well-discussed posts about open source.
