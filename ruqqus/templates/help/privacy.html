How often pages are asked for

{{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} counts how many requests its pages get, on our own server and with no outside service. For each day (in UTC) it adds one to three totals: one for the kind of page ("a post", "a profile"), one for the site that linked to it (its name only, never the address of the page you were on, and "this site" or "none" when that is what it was), and one for the country, where the network in front of the site tells us. Pages a script fetches for itself and pages in a side panel aren't counted. The totals are kept for 400 days and only site admins see them.

Ads

{{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} shows ads from Google AdSense on feeds and post pages and in the right sidebar beside them. Settings, chat, sign-in, search and help pages have none, and neither do a shared draft's pages.

We check what is written on a page before ads go on it.
