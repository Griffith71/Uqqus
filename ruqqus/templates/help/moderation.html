{{ _("How moderation is recorded") }}

{{ _("The Log is one record of what guildmasters and the site decided, and of what members deleted. What it says is never rewritten: a correction is a new line that points at the one it corrects. Only three things are taken out of a line, and each leaves the line itself: a reason an administrator hides after a report, the item a line pointed at when its author later takes their name off, and the words of something an administrator erases. Open it from the footer, or at /log.") }}

{{ _("What the Log shows") }}

  • {{ _("Moderation: a post or comment hidden or removed, restored, or hidden in a version. For each one: who (a guildmaster by name; the site's admins as \"Site admin\", never by name), when, where, which rule, and the reason written for it. It never shows the title or the text of what was hidden.") }}
  • {{ _("Sanctions: exiles, chat mutes, suspensions and bans, with the rule and the reason.") }}
  • {{ _("Guild changes: what a guild's guildmasters changed in how it is run.") }}
  • {{ _("Deleted: what members deleted, as Anonymous (below).") }}
  • {{ _("Edits: what members edited: who (Anonymous for an anonymous post or comment), when and the note they gave. A run of edits to one item is one line; the versions themselves are on the item's own history page. An edit in the first five minutes after something was written is part of writing it and isn't recorded, and an account can edit 30 times an hour.") }}

{{ _("A private guild's decisions are read only by its own members and the site's admins, and the guild is never named in the public Log. What the site does to run itself (its defences, its lists, its bots) is read by the admins only. The site's automatic checks write generic lines: \"Automatic spam check\", \"Automatic image check\".") }}

{{ _("When you delete something") }}

{{ _("Deleting a post or comment has two ways, and you choose in the dialog:") }}

  • {{ _("Delete it. It leaves the site. What it said (the title, the link and the text) stays in the Log as Anonymous: no name, no profile and no link, and a link in it is shown as plain text. Anyone can read it there, under the same word filter, sensitive-content and Child-level rules as any member's words. It is how a record of the site stays honest: like Wikipedia, every contribution remains.") }}
  • {{ _("Remove my name. The post or comment stays where it is, with your name taken off, and leaves your profile. It can't be undone, and it isn't offered for a post made for a Circle or a post that has been tipped. On a post written with others it takes off your own credit only.") }}

{{ _("The first five minutes. If you delete something less than five minutes after you made it, and nobody else has commented on it, it is really erased: nothing is published and nothing is in the Log. The site keeps a private copy for 90 days, which only you and the site's admins can open. This is for a typo, a wrong picture or a post made in anger. It can be used 20 times a day; after that, and after five minutes, a delete is published as above.") }}

{{ _("Comments under a deleted post stay. They are read under the post in the Log, where the post is Anonymous. The comments you wrote under your own post become Anonymous with it. A comment its own author deletes is shown as Anonymous there too.") }}

{{ _("What is never published: the words of an item a moderator had removed or a guild had hidden, of an item in a banned guild, and of anything deleted by an account locked as a child's: those show a line and no words. A post made for a Circle, or in a private guild, is kept for that audience only.") }}

{{ _("Many at once. In History > Manage you can tick up to 100 of your posts and comments and remove your name from them, or delete them, in one go. It asks for your password (and your two-factor code if you have one), and does exactly what the dialog does for each one: what can't be changed, such as a post for a Circle, is listed afterwards with the reason. History > Anonymous > Deleted lists what you deleted yourself, from the copies kept for you.") }}

{{ _("When you delete your account. Everything you wrote becomes Anonymous and stays on the site, where it was, instead of showing a deleted account. Nothing is removed, and it can't be undone. Your private copies of what you erased yourself are emptied.") }}

{{ _("Anonymous #3. In one thread, everyone who is anonymous there has a number, given in the order they became anonymous there (when they posted or commented anonymously, or when a delete, \"Remove my name\" or closing their account made it so). It is the same number on a post and on that person's anonymous comments, a different one in every other thread, and it stays when an account is deleted. It tells people apart; it never says who they are.") }}

{{ _("Exiling or muting \"Anonymous #3\". A guild's guildmasters can exile an anonymous person from the guild, or mute them in its channels, by their number. The guildmaster is never told whose it is: the site applies it to the account behind the number, and the guild's lists, its mod log and the Log show only \"Anonymous #3\". The person it was done to is told, can open the Log entry and can appeal it. A guildmaster who already suspects a particular account can still test that guess, so this keeps who it is from casual view, not from a determined guildmaster.") }}

{{ _("Posts written with others. Each author's credit can be anonymous on its own: when the post is anonymous from the start, when an author chooses \"Remove my name\" for their credit, or when they delete their account. Only that credit becomes \"Anonymous #N\"; the other authors stay named. Admins can always see whose it is.") }}

{{ _("Bans that cite what someone wrote. When an admin bans or suspends an account they may cite the posts and comments that justify it, even ones written anonymously. Once a second admin agrees (or, on a site with a single administrator, its owner), the Log names the account for those items (the line about each one, and the ban's line), so anyone can check the ban was fair. The items stay anonymous everywhere else on the site, nothing is named without that approval, and once it is named it stays: if the ban is later reversed, or a suspension simply runs out, the Log shows that too and keeps the name, so a ban that was found wrong can be seen as such.") }}

{{ _("The site's admins can always see who wrote something: anonymity is toward other members, not toward the site.") }}

{{ _("An exile needs a reason and something it relies on. A guildmaster who exiles someone writes why and names at least one of that person's own posts or comments in the guild that the exile relies on; a chat mute needs a reason too, and an admin's takedown always does. The site keeps a private copy of each item that was named, as it was, for a year: the person, the guild's guildmasters and the admins can open it, so a later edit or delete can't rewrite what the decision rested on. The Log lists a named, public item by its id.") }}

{{ _("When something is lifted you are told. If an exile or a mute ends, a hidden or removed post or comment is shown again, a suspension runs out or a banned guild is reinstated, the people it concerned are sent a short notice. A chat mute and a guild's ban are told when they are made, with the rule, the reason and an Appeal button, as other decisions are; a guild's founder and full guildmasters can each appeal its ban.") }}

{{ _("Trying to find out who is behind an anonymous account. Guessing, testing or comparing accounts to unmask someone who chose to be anonymous is unfair exposure, and it can lead to a ban. The site can see when a guildmaster acts on an account both by number and by name, and its staff are told so they can look.") }}

{{ _("Removing an anonymous author of a shared post. The authors of a shared post can propose removing an anonymous author by their number. The other authors never see whose number it is while they vote. If the vote passes, the author is removed and so everyone knows who it was; their credit on the post stays a number.") }}

{{ _("Asking for words to come down") }}

{{ _("Every Log entry that shows someone's words, whether a deleted item's text or a reason, has a Report this button. The site's admins read it, and they are never told who reported. They can erase the words everywhere, including every copy kept in the Log, or hide a reason. Use it for harassment, someone's personal details, text you own the copyright of, or a request to have your own words removed. Guildmasters have no tool for deleted items; a rule-breaking comment is reported and an admin decides.") }}

{{ _("Private copies") }}

{{ _("When something is hidden, removed or erased, the site keeps a private copy of its text for 90 days (a year for illegal content, which the law requires). Who may open it depends on what it is a copy of: its author and the admins, and for a guild's hide that guild's guildmasters. Then the text is emptied; the Log's line stays.") }}

{{ _("Automatic checks") }}

{{ _("An automatic check can put an account on hold. It can never ban one. The site checks for spam, for links it blocks, for spam in a profile, for someone getting round a ban, and for pictures that match a list. When one of them fires it puts the account on hold (the Log calls it a quarantine) and a member of the staff looks at it. What follows is always a person's decision: they release the account, or an admin bans it, with a reason, a rule and an appeal, as described above.") }}

{{ _("What a held account can do. It can read the site, change its own settings and profile, delete what it wrote, bookmark, unfollow, leave guilds and send feedback. It can't post, comment, edit, vote, follow someone, join a guild, forward, repost, tip, report, use chat or calls, write in a channel, a wiki or an event, make a Space, a circle, a curation or a story, or use a guildmaster's powers. The hold itself takes nothing down.") }}

{{ _("Nobody else is told. There is no banner on its profile, and no page names an account that is on hold. The account is sent a notice with a generic reason (and the rule, where there is one) and a link to ask for a review.") }}

{{ _("What the Log shows. Most holds have one line on the Sanctions tab, from the site: \"put an account in quarantine\", with the rule, a generic reason (\"Automatic spam check\", \"Automatic link check\" or \"Automatic image check\") and the time. It names no account, no post and no guild, and says nothing about what was caught. When such a hold is lifted a second line says \"lifted a quarantine an automatic check had placed\". The staff keep a separate note that names the account and says what the check saw; only they read it. Some holds have no public line and are read by the staff only, like the rest of what the site does to run itself.") }}

{{ _("What an automatic check removes is public. When an automatic check removes a post or comment, the Log shows it on the Moderation tab like any removal: the post or comment by its id, the rule and the generic reason, never its title or text and never who wrote it, even when it was anonymous. The removal itself sends no separate notice: the person it was by finds an Appeal this decision link on the removed post or comment, and the site's admins decide that appeal.") }}

{{ _("Only a person lifts a hold. Not time, and not verifying an email address: an admin releases it from the staff queue, or accepts a request for a review. A hold the sign-up check puts on a brand-new account is different: verifying an email address lifts that one, unless an automatic check has fired on the account since.") }}

{{ _("Asking for a review. The notice you are sent has an Ask for a review link, which opens /appeal/quarantine (you need to be signed in). You can ask once for each hold, with up to 500 characters, and the site's admins decide it, never the check that held you. If they accept, the hold is lifted, a new line in the Log says so and you are told; if not, you are told that too. If an admin later bans the account, that is a separate decision with its own reason, rule and appeal, and what is said above about bans that cite anonymous posts applies to it. A hold never names an account for something it wrote anonymously.") }}

{{ _("If a decision was wrong") }}

{{ _("When a hide, a removal, an exile, a suspension or a ban is about you, you are sent the rule, the reason and a link to its line in the Log, with an Appeal button. You can appeal each decision once. The guild's guildmasters decide an appeal against a guild's decision; the site's admins decide the rest. If it is reversed, a new line in the Log says so.") }}

{{ _("A hold an automatic check put on your account is asked about the same way, once for each hold: see Automatic checks above.") }}
