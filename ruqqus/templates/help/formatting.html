Links to other sites

When a post links to a video, a song or a post on another site, the post shows it:

  • Videos play in the post: YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, Kick, Rumble, Odysee, BitChute, Bilibili, Streamable, Loom and TED; Facebook videos and reels, Instagram reels and TikTok videos play when you press play.
  • Music and podcasts: Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Apple Podcasts, Bandcamp, Mixcloud and Deezer show their player; nothing plays until you press it.
  • GIFs and photos: GIPHY, Tenor and Flickr.
  • Posts on X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, Instagram, Reddit, Tumblr, Pinterest, Telegram and Truth Social show as a card with who wrote it and what they wrote. A Facebook post shows the page it is from; its text stays on Facebook.
