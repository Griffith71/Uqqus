Roles

A role is a coloured name beside someone's messages in +{{ b.name }}'s channels. It can let its people skip slow mode, remove or pin messages, and on each channel (under Channels) allow or deny reading and writing over the channel's own rules. Where one role allows and another denies, allowing wins. A role never lets someone into a channel of a guild they can't see, and never changes what a guildmaster can do. Up to {{ max_roles }}.
