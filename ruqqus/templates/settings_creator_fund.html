The rules

  • A point needs an account at least {{ rules.ACCOUNT_AGE_DAYS }} days old that is not banned or deleted. Your own votes and comments never count.
  • One account gives you at most {{ rules.POINTS_PER_ACCOUNT }} points a month, however much it votes and comments.
  • Only public posts and comments count: not Circle posts or posts in private guilds, and not anything removed or deleted.
  • Anonymous posts and comments count for you like any other. Your points and earnings are shown only to you; the site's admins see the payout list.
  • Nobody gets more than {{ (rules.MAX_SHARE * 100) | int }}% of a month's budget. Shares are rounded down to whole coins.
  • Votes or comments from accounts that are paid, fake, or run by you or for you are not allowed. A payout that relies on them can be held, and you can be taken out of the fund.
  • To be paid you must be taking part, have an account at least {{ rules.ACCOUNT_AGE_DAYS }} days old, and owe nothing to the site.
  • A month is paid after it ends, in coins added to your balance. You get a notification.
