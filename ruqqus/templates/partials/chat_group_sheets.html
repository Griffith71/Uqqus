New chat

Someone who follows you gets your message in their Inbox; anyone else gets it as a request. Chats are end-to-end encrypted.

New channel
Who can join

You and up to 3 helpers write; members read and react. For other circles, make it on your channels page. You can have up to 3 channels.

New group

People who follow you, or whom you follow, are in at once. Anyone else gets an invitation in their Requests. A group has up to {{ chat_group_max }} people and is end-to-end encrypted.
