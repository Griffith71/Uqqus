      {% call rule(prefix, 1, "Free expression and good faith") %}

      Good-faith discussion, disagreement and criticism are permitted. Deliberate deception and bad-faith conduct intended to mislead or harm others are prohibited.

      Claims of deliberate deception need clear, documented evidence: disagreement, unpopular opinions or speculation about motives are not enough on their own.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 2, "Criminal activity and prohibited conduct") %}

      Do not use {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} to engage in, facilitate or promote criminal activity or conduct prohibited by these rules. This includes harassment, scams (including gambling schemes), fraud, spam, unsolicited commercial promotion, violence, malware, phishing, cyberattacks and other unlawful activity.

      Do not infringe copyright or other intellectual property rights. Do not copy, repost or redistribute protected content without permission or a valid legal basis, or link to pirated copies. Do not falsely claim authorship of another person's work.

      Do not use deceptive impersonation of other users, public figures, organisations or {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} staff, and do not publish personal information, yours or anyone else's.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 3, "Content standards") %}

      Content consisting entirely of any one of the following is prohibited:

      • erotic material
      • violent material
      • grotesque material

      A broader work is not prohibited merely because it contains erotic, violent or grotesque elements.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 4, "Transparency and disclosure") %}

      Clearly label sensitive content. Disclose commercial promotion, including paid partnerships and other material commercial relationships. Identify content created using AI. Automated accounts must clearly identify themselves as bots.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 5, "Evading and manipulating the site") %}

      Do not evade a {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} ban or suspension, or use another account to circumvent a block imposed by another user. No hacking, no trying to get into accounts or systems that are not yours, and no exploiting a flaw for advantage: if you find a problem, tell us.

      No manipulating votes, and no fake or coordinated accounts that give a false impression of support or activity.

      Anonymous posts and comments follow the same rules as everything else. Anonymity hides who wrote them from other users, not from {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }}.
