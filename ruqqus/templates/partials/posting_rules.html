      {% call rule(prefix, 1, "Free speech, not bad faith") %}

      Free speech does not protect insincere or bad-faith expression: speech driven by undisclosed, harmful motives.

      Harassment, scams (including gambling schemes), misinformation, spam and violence are banned, and so is any promotion of them or of a group or person complicit in them.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 2, "No illegal content") %}

      Intellectual property. Copyright is protected. Do not post copyrighted material you are not authorized to distribute, or content that spreads it, such as links to pirated copies.

      Unsafe products and services. Selling or promoting unsafe or unregulated products or services is banned.

      Do not post anything that is not legal to publish within, or export from, the United States of America.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 3, "General anti-degen policy") %}

      Content made purely for entertainment is banned when it is:

      • purely erotic material
      • purely violent material
      • purely grotesque material
      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 4, "No unnecessary duplicate posting") %}

      Check that what you are posting has not been posted before. Posting the same thing again without good reason is banned.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 5, "Personal information and impersonation") %}

      Do not share or publish personal information, yours or anyone else's. Do not impersonate other people or {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} staff.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 6, "Protect minors") %}

      Sexual or sexually suggestive material involving anyone under 18, including fictitious content, is never allowed, whatever label it carries.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 7, "Digital safety") %}

      No malware, phishing or other attacks, and no URL shorteners.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 8, "Be upfront") %}

      Mark sensitive content as sensitive. Disclose paid partnerships and content made with AI. Bots must identify themselves as bots.

      {% endcall %} {% call rule(prefix, 9, "Play fair") %}

      No evading bans or blocks with other accounts, no vote manipulation, and no coordinated harassment.

      Anonymous posts and comments follow the same rules. Other users cannot see who wrote them, but {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }} can.
