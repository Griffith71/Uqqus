Read and write: your chats, groups, channels for circles and encrypted guild channels.

Read only: public channels and other guild channels. Their messages go through the site's word filter and rules, so a message written there from an app is removed: write those on this site.

On this site only: starting a chat, accepting a message request, making a group or adding people, calls and Spaces. An app can't find people, make rooms or start a call on this server: the site keeps who can reach you as it does here.

Not yet: pictures and files. The chat server keeps no files, so an app can't send one.
