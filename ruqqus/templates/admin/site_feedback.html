Feedback

What members send from "Send feedback" in the account menu (helpers/site_feedback.py). Mark an item seen, fixed or declined; a response you write is sent to the member as a notification, once when it is new, and again when you close the item. A screenshot is deleted a month after the item is closed, and everything goes with the member's account. Members never see each other's feedback.
