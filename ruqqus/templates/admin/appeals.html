Appeals

Appeals of decisions the site's admins or its own checks made (a removal, a suspension, a ban or a quarantine) and of a post author's hide of a comment. A guild's own decisions are decided by that guild's guildmasters. An appeal is private: its text is read only here. Reversed runs the undo and writes a new entry in the Log linked to the original (the original is never edited); Upheld means it stands; Dismissed means it was not taken further. Say why in a line: the person is sent it. You can't decide an appeal of your own decision while another admin could.
