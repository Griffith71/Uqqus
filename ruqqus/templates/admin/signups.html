Sign-ups

The signup guard (helpers/signup_risk.py) refuses a sign-up on a hard sign of a bot, holds one whose soft signs add up to the threshold, and lets the rest through. A held account can read and change its settings but can't post, comment, vote, follow, tip, report or start a chat until you approve it here or it verifies an email that isn't a throwaway. Nothing here names an address: a network is a keyed hash of its /24 or /64.
