What's happening

The box in the right sidebar and the /happening page show trending topics and threads, what is live, and the calendar. Everything on this page is entered by hand: scores, polls and predictions, countdowns, elections, and alerts and news that link out to their source. Times are UTC. Holidays, observances, equinoxes, moon phases and eclipses need no entry: the site works them out.
