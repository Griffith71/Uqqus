Reports of words in the Log

Members can report a Log entry that shows someone's words: what a member deleted, which the Log keeps as Anonymous, or a reason written for a decision. You are never told who reported. Purge erases the words everywhere through the same path as the purge button, the Log's copies included (irreversible). Hide the reason (level 4) hides a reason from everyone below staff and writes a new entry that says so. Dismiss closes the report. One decision closes every open report of an entry. Guildmasters have no tool for deleted items.
