Community note requests

Members ask for a community note from the flag menu when a post or comment may be misleading or needs context. A request is not a policy flag. Write a note only when it helps: it is shown under the post or comment to everyone, signed by the moderators (never by you), and the people who asked are told. Otherwise dismiss the requests. A request on a forwarded copy counts for the post it was copied from, and the note shows on every copy.
