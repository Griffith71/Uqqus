Quarantine

The site's own checks (the spam guard, a banned or malicious link, the SEO-spam check, ban-evasion detection and the image checks) never ban or suspend an account: they put it on hold. A held account can read the site and change its own settings, and can't post, comment, vote, follow, tip, report or use chat or calls (its chat devices are signed out). You decide what follows. Release lifts the hold; the person is told, and an appeal of it that is still waiting is settled as reversed. To ban it, open its profile and use the usual ban form there (a reveal of an anonymous poster still needs a second admin); a banned account leaves this list. Nothing public names a held account: the Log has one line that names nobody, and the entry that names the account is the staff's. An email never releases a hold of this kind, and neither does time. An appeal waits at Appeals.
