Bots

Members apply for bot accounts in Settings > Bots (helpers/bots.py). A bot posts the title and link of each new item of its approved feeds, on its own profile; its profile says who runs it. Approve a bot only when its feeds are what its owner says and the source allows reuse of its headlines (for example the BBC asks for attribution and no BBC logos). Approving a bot approves the feeds it applied with; a feed added later waits here on its own.
