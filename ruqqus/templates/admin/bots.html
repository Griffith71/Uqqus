Bots

Bots are proposed in Settings > Bots (helpers/bots.py). A site bot runs on free feeds: once approved it belongs to nobody, says "Automated by {{ 'SITE_NAME' | app_config }}" and only admins change it. A member bot needs its member to run it (a key for their own script, or a paid source): it stays theirs and says "Automated by @member". Open a proposal to change its name, picture, banner, bio, feeds, guilds or kind before you approve it. Approve a bot only when its feeds are what the proposal says and the source allows reuse of its headlines (for example the BBC asks for attribution and no BBC logos). Approving a bot approves the feeds it was proposed with; a feed a member adds later waits here on its own.
