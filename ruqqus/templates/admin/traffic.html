What is counted: a successful GET of a page of one of the kinds above, which is not every page: guildmaster tools, edit forms, the history and revisions of a post or comment and a guild's moderation log aren't on the list, and a new page counts for nothing until it is added. Never the API, this admin area, a page in a side panel, what a script fetches for itself, a prefetch or anything on the site's list of robots. Reloads count. Days are UTC. Sign-ups leave out bot accounts; posts leave out bots' posts, forwarded copies, forwarded comments and Spaces, and include what was later removed or deleted.
