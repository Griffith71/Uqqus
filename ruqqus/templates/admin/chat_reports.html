Reported chat messages

Private chats (1:1 chats and groups) are end-to-end encrypted: the site can't read them. A member who reports a message sends it and up to four before it, as their own browser shows them. The site checked with the chat server that each of these messages is in that chat, and who sent it and when come from the chat server; the words can't be checked, so they are the reporter's copy. The page doesn't say who reported (though the reporter's own messages among these carry their name, as every message does), and the reported person is never told. The copied words are emptied 90 days after a report is closed.
