# Platform audit, November 2026

Audited 2026-10-17 against the working tree of branch `Laba-v0.1`: commit `b9220c23d` plus about 3,650 uncommitted lines across 55 files and 52 new files. Nothing was fixed when it was written; section 13 records where each finding stands on 2026-10-21.

This audit builds on two earlier ones:
- `BACKEND_AUDIT_FINDINGS.md`: passes from 2026-09-18 to 10-11;
- `AUDIT_2026-10.md`: the feature audit of 2026-10-16.

Their findings are re-checked here and carried forward, not re-derived. The work concentrates on everything built since 10-16, none of which had been audited:
- embed players;
- Reddit-style live threads;
- guild flair, wiki, resources and events;
- shared drafts and collaborative posts;
- `users.last_active_utc`;
- the `edit_post` / `delete_post` refactor.

It also re-runs the system-wide checks (crawl, query counts, accessibility, tests, dependencies) across the whole platform.

## 1. Summary

**Nothing from October has been fixed, and the most serious items were re-confirmed live today:**
- **$-01.** Five parallel tips of 400 coins from a 499-coin balance all succeeded: 2,000 coins were recorded, only 400 were paid, so 1,600 coins were created.
- **X-01.** A rotating forged `X-Forwarded-For` beat the login rate limiter 80 times out of 80.
- **E-01.** Every post embed still answers 500.
- **I-02.** A login with no password still crashes.

The money, chat-consent and relay findings (M-01, M-02, M-05, $-02, $-03) sit in files untouched since October.

**The new features mostly hold up.**
- In live tests, shared drafts converged and credited every character correctly with two browsers typing at once, including through an offline stretch.
- Required flair, wiki edit levels and private-guild gates behaved.
- A crawl of 310 GET URLs as 5 viewers and an empty-body POST to all 32 new state-changing routes found no new 500s.

**The problems are at the edges, where the new features meet the old ones:**

1. **Collaborative posts can be orphaned or doubled.**
   - When the last author leaves, the post can never again be edited or deleted by anyone, and its draft is gone (C-01).
   - Two authors pressing Publish at once create two posts (C-02).
   - Whoever published first keeps controls the others don't have, such as comment permission and forwarding (C-03).
2. **Live updates stop silently.** A member who opens a few pages quickly hits the 6-stream cap, and the browser never retries. That page then gets no live events at all, including incoming call rings (L-05).
3. **Guild rules have side doors.**
   - Forwarding a comment ignores required flair (G-01), and a bot's posts to such a guild vanish without a trace (G-02).
   - Two simultaneous wiki saves silently lose one of them (G-03).
   - Wiki page titles and resource labels skip the word filter (G-04).
4. **Performance.** A guild page now runs 312 queries for a signed-in member, about 11 per post card (P-06).

**Counts.** New: 0 S0, 3 S1 (C-01, C-02, L-05), 9 S2, 8 S3. Carried forward and still open: everything October listed except two fixes, so by October's own counts 1 S0, 9 S1, 19 S2 and about 24 S3. Fixed since October: L-02 (S3) and X-03 (S2). One got worse: D-05 has more published advisories against the same versions.

## 5. Findings by severity (new)

### S1

**C-01. When a collaborative post's last author leaves, the post is locked forever.** Confirmed.

`collab_leave` closes the draft when no authors are left (`routes/collab.py`, `collab_leave`: `store.close`). But the post stays tied to the draft:
- `edit_post` refuses any post with a draft row (`routes/posts.py`, `collab_store.doc_for_post(...) is not None`, which matches closed drafts too);
- `delete_post_pid` refuses it too (`COLLAB_DELETE_REFUSAL`).

Live, with a two-author draft published and then both authors leaving:

> doc closed: t | author edit: 403 | author delete: 403 | draft reachable: 404 | fork still offered: 200

**Impact.** Nobody but an admin can ever change or delete that post, not even the person whose profile it is on. The same happens to any author who leaves by mistake.

**Fix direction.** Either:
- keep the last author from leaving a published draft (they must delete the post by proposal, or hand it over);
- or, when the draft closes, release the post to its profile owner (drop the refusal for closed drafts).

**C-02. Two authors pressing Publish at the same moment create two posts.** Confirmed.

`collab_publish` reads `doc.post_id` without a lock (`routes/collab.py`, `_publish`: `post = ... if doc[7] else None`, then `_post_as(... "/api/vue/submit" ...)`). Two simultaneous requests both see no post and both submit. Live result: answers `[200, 500]`, and two submissions titled "Race publish" (ids 278 and 279). The 500 is `UniqueViolation: collab_publishes_number_key` in `store.add_publish`.

**Impact.**
- The extra post is an ordinary post, not linked to the draft: no collaborative protection, no "Written together", no co-authors.
- The person who caused it sees an error.

**Fix direction.** `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the draft row (`store.doc(lock=True)`), held across the first publish, or a "publishing" claim column. Then make the second request answer "Already published".

**L-05. A page that hits the live-stream cap never gets live updates, including call rings.** Confirmed (observed) and Traced.

- **The server.** `routes/live_events.py:54` answers **429** when a member has 6 streams open on a worker. A stream's server side lingers until the next keepalive notices the client is gone (up to 25 s).
- **When it happens.** The site reloads on every page and each page opens a stream, so opening about 6 pages in under half a minute is enough. Playwright reproduced it twice in 16 page loads (`failed: 429 /api/live/events`).
- **The client.** `assets/js/live_events.js` has no error handling. Per the EventSource standard, a non-200 answer closes the stream for good (no reconnect).

**Impact.**
- On that page: no incoming-call ring (`call_ring.js`), no live comments, no Space roster changes, no "new updates" in live threads.
- The collaborative editor falls back to polling every 8 s; nothing else does.

**Fix direction.**
- Close the previous stream when the page unloads (`pagehide` → `source.close()`).
- Count streams with a short TTL in Redis instead of per worker.
- In the client, back off and reopen after an `error` while `readyState` is `CLOSED`.

### S2

**C-03. Whoever published a collaborative post first keeps powers the other authors don't have.** Confirmed.

The post's `author_id` is the first publisher. Every route that checks `post.author_id == v.id` still treats them as sole owner. Live:
- the first publisher changed "Who can comment" to *No one* alone (`POST /api/post/7t/comment_permission` → 200);
- the other author got 403.

The same applies, by the code, to:
- forwarding to guilds (`forward_post`): only the author forwards;
- starting a live thread (`live_toggle`, which allows co-authors too);
- post insights;
- what happens after a block (P-01).

Changing the audience to circles was refused, but only because an older rule forbids co-authors on a Circle post.

**Impact.** It contradicts the governance the drafts promise ("decided together"), and the other authors can't forward their own post.

**Fix direction.** Either:
- route these controls through the draft (a proposal kind, or "any author" as the rules say);
- or state plainly in the draft that publishing makes you the post's owner for these settings.

**G-01. Forwarding a comment ignores a guild's required flair.** Confirmed.

The required-flair check exists in `forward_post` (`routes/posts.py`, `flair_rules.forward_refusal`) and in `submit_post` (`COMPOSER_REQUIRED`), but not in `forward_comment` (`routes/comments.py:1020`). Live: with flair required in +gfpub, `POST /comment/<id>/forward board=gfpub` answered 200, and an unflaired post appeared in the guild.

**Fix direction.** The same check in `forward_comment`, plus a flair picker in its dialog.

**G-02. A bot's posts to a guild that requires flair disappear without a trace.** Traced.

`submit_post` refuses a composer forward to a flair-requiring guild with **409**. `helpers/bot_runner.classify` maps every 409 to `"duplicate"` (`bot_runner.py:32-33`), so the item is marked seen and never posted, neither on the profile nor in the guild. Nobody is told.

**Fix direction.** A distinct refusal code (or `bot_refusal: "guild"`) so the runner posts again without forwarding, as it already does for other guild refusals.

**G-03. Two people saving a wiki page at once: one save is silently lost.** Confirmed.

`guild_wiki_save` has no base revision; it only skips a save whose text equals the current text. Live: a guild founder and an approved member saved additions to the same page at once. Both answered 200, and the current text kept only one addition ("line one / Circle's addition"). Both revisions are in the history, so it can be recovered, but nobody is warned.

**Fix direction.** Send the revision the editor started from. Refuse, or show a merge, when it is no longer current (Wikipedia's edit conflict).

**C-04. Deleted and banned authors still count as voters in a shared draft.** Traced.

- `store.authors` lists every author whose `left_utc = 0`, with no check on `is_deleted` or ban (`helpers/collab_store.py`, `authors`).
- `delete_account` (`routes/settings.py:486`) doesn't touch `collab_authors` (part of the broader I-05).
- A banned author can't vote (`is_not_banned` on `collab_vote`) but still counts.

**Impact.** Under the default unanimous rule a deleted account blocks every decision until its `last_active_utc` is 6 months old. With "never" chosen as the inactivity window, the block is permanent.

**Fix direction.** Leave deleted accounts out of `counted`; end their authorship in `delete_account`; decide what a ban means.

**G-04. Wiki page titles and pinned-resource labels skip the word filter.** Traced.

- Wiki severity is computed from the body only (`routes/guild_wiki.py:235`, `_severity(body)`).
- Resource labels are never rated (`guild_wiki_store.add_resource`).

Titles show in the page list, the page heading and the history; labels show on every guild page, to every reader including Child-filter viewers. Flair names and event titles are rated.

**Fix direction.** Rate the title with the body. Rate labels (or reuse the flair name rules).

**P-06. A guild page runs about 11 queries per post card.** Confirmed.

Measured as a signed-in member on `/+news` (28 cards): **312 statements**; as a visitor, 168.

| Page | Visitor | Member |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | 77 | 134 |
| `/all` | 77 | 121 |
| `/+news` | 168 | 312 |
| `/@audit_author` | 65 | 105 |
| `/search?q=post` | 154 | 152 |
| `/post/7t` | 20 | 53 |

The top repeats:
- the same `SELECT submissions ...` 120 times, 4 per card, from the flair and menu partials;
- `SELECT forwardrels ...` once per card. That one already existed in October; it comes from the menus' forwarder checks.
- **New:** `may_set_flair` runs `_forwarder_id`, a `ForwardRelationship` query, and is called twice per card: menu item and phone sheet (`routes/guild_flair.py:144-201`).

**Fix direction.** Attach the forwarder and the menu state per page in one query (as `flair_store.attach` does for flair), and compute `may_set_flair` once per card.

**D-08. Keystroke records, live updates and view counts are kept forever.** Traced.

Nothing deletes from:
- `collab_ops`: every batch of characters an author typed, including text they deleted before publishing, kept after they leave or delete their account;
- `live_updates`;
- `post_view_days`.

Only `bot_seen` has a retention job (`bot_store.py:284`).

**Fix direction.** Prune or compact `collab_ops` once a draft is published, or after N days (the authorship runs already keep the credit). Set retention for the others. Include them in account deletion and data export (I-05, I-06).

**W-01. The `web/` CI job now fails its own audit gate.** Confirmed.

`.github/workflows/ci.yml:45` runs `npm audit --omit=dev --audit-level=high`. `web/` pins `next` 16.3.6, which has two high advisories:
- "Pending `use cache` fill can leak Draft Mode content";
- "cache poisoning of SSG and ISR pages".

There is also a high advisory in `source-map-js`. A fix is available (`next` 16.4.2, not a major version).

**D-05 (carried forward, worse).** The same installed versions as October now carry more published advisories (table in section 3). Upgrade path: aiohttp ≥ 3.14.3, Pillow ≥ 12.3.0, urllib3 ≥ 2.8.0, cryptography ≥ 49, werkzeug ≥ 3.1.9, flask ≥ 3.1.3, requests ≥ 2.33.0, bleach ≥ 6.4.0, markdown ≥ 3.8.1.

## 7. UX and accessibility

- **Accessibility (axe, serious and critical).** The site-wide issues of U-04 are unchanged on every page checked: unnamed icon links (30 on the home feed), images without `alt` (29), an unnamed progress bar and command, low contrast in the shared theme colours. Settings > Profile still has 3 unlabelled selects and one unlabelled field. The new pages add none of their own beyond inheriting the shell.
- **Two "drafts" systems.** Post drafts (Save draft / Schedule) and shared drafts (`/collab`) use the same word in the account menu and the composer, with no explanation of the difference. They don't share a list.
- **Two co-author systems.** A co-authored post (up to 5, the author edits) and a collaborative post (up to 50, everyone edits) look the same on the byline. The post menu offers "Co-authors" on one and "Open the shared draft" on the other.
- **Guild tabs appear only when there's content.** A member can't discover that a guild could have a wiki or events. Guildmasters see the tab with "(off)".
- **Chat.** `/chat` logs a 404 for `room_keys/version` on every load (no key backup: M-03) and for an unused Matrix RTC probe.
- **Brand.** "Ruqqus" still appears beside the "laba" logo (U-02); unchanged.

## 8. Architecture, performance, data integrity, tests

- **Queries.** See P-06.
  - The home feed's one-`forwardrels`-per-card pattern predates the new work.
  - Memoized work repeats in dev because caching is off there.
  - Production behaviour depends on the cache. Hypothesis: a guild page for members stays above 200 statements because the per-card queries aren't memoized.
- **Nested in-process requests.** The draft scheduler, the bot runner and collaborative publishing post through the real routes in this process. Publishing needs its own greenlet: sessions are greenlet-local, and an inner request in the same greenlet closed the outer request's session (found and fixed during the build). Any new caller must do the same. There is one shared helper pattern but no shared helper (`routes/collab._post_as` vs `scripts/publish_scheduled.publish`).
- **Per-worker TTL caches.** `coauthors._live_ids`, `community_notes`, `collab_view`: permissions and labels lag across workers by up to 30 s. `collab_view` patches this for posts younger than 60 s only.
- **Schema.** D-01 and D-02 are unchanged.
  - None of the 13 migrations since 10-20 adds a foreign key to `users`. That is by design, but it means every account deletion leaves orphans to clean by hand (I-05).
  - New tables have indexes for their main queries (event range by `board_id, starts_utc`; ops by `doc_id, revision`).
- **Background jobs.**
  - `scripts/recomputes.py`: each new step is wrapped in its own `try`, but the long post re-score at the top isn't. A database error there (seen: `DiskFull`) kills the cycle, and supervisord restarts it.
  - Output is buffered (no `flush`), so the job's progress doesn't show in the logs until it exits.
- **Tests.**
  - 166 test files: **68 only read source text**; 98 execute code.
  - Only the vote and voter routes run through the route harness (`tests/legacy_harness.py`). **No route of any feature built since October is ever executed by a test.**
  - No test runs against Postgres: row locks (`FOR UPDATE`), partial indexes and advisory locks are untested.
  - There is no concurrency test anywhere, which is how $-01, $-02 and C-02 got through.
  - JavaScript is tested only through the OT vectors.
  - **T-01**: the highest-value missing tests are concurrency tests on every coin path and on publishing; route tests of collab, wiki and events against Postgres in CI; and a render test of every template (E-01 would have been caught).
  - **T-02**: `edit_post` and `delete_post_pid` were split into `apply_post_edit` and `delete_post_as` without the characterisation tests the repo rules ask for. They were checked by hand live (edit, a stranger's refused edit, delete, the history row), not by a test.

## 9. `web/` (light pass)

- **State.** It is the Next.js starter page plus Auth.js (GitHub and Google OAuth, database sessions through the Prisma adapter) on its own Supabase database: models `User`, `Account`, `Session`, `VerificationToken`. It has no features, no API routes beyond auth, and shares no data or accounts with the legacy app.
- **Security basics.**
  - Secrets come from env (`.env.example` documents them).
  - Sessions are database-backed.
  - `ratelimit.ts` reads Upstash credentials with non-null assertions: any import crashes without them. It is unused today.
  - Dependencies: W-01.
- **CI.** The web job runs typecheck, lint, format, unit and e2e tests and a high-level audit. The audit gate now fails (W-01).
- **The decision the owner needs to make** (open since October): whether `web/` is the future of the product, and if so how accounts and content move across from the legacy database. Until then it is cost without benefit: CI time, dependency advisories, a second auth system.

## 10. Missing features and opportunities (ranked: value to members against cost)

1. **Fix before building.** The S0/S1 list is the highest-value work: money integrity, chat consent, the rate limiter, collaborative-post orphaning, live-update loss.
2. **Account deletion and data export across every feature** (I-05, I-06, C-04, D-08). This is a legal and trust prerequisite.
3. **One moderation queue with appeals.** Reports, flags, story, user, broadcast and Space reports and note requests live in six separate admin pages.
4. **Notifications outside the open tab** (web push), with per-feature preferences. Draft proposals, events, calls and live threads all depend on someone having a page open.
5. **Edit conflicts and merges for wiki pages** (G-03), and **live cursors** for shared drafts.
6. **An API for the new features.** Drafts, wiki, events and flair have no `/api/v1` or `/api/v2` endpoints; bots and third-party apps can't use them.
7. **Recurring events and event comments**; wiki search; flair-based automod (auto-flair, or "require flair from new members only").
8. **An installable app** (the manifest still points at `ruqqus.com`: U-02).
9. **Decide `web/`** (section 9).

## 11. What this audit could not assess

- **Production.** Cloudflare and the origin firewall the relay depends on; real env values; S3 policies; which migrations a live database has; real data volumes and load.
- **Third-party services.** Google Drive, YouTube, OneDrive, Dailymotion, Twitch, PayPal, AdSense, email. Social embeds from a real network (TikTok timed out from the container before).
- **Real devices and networks.** Safari and iOS, WebRTC across real NATs, E2EE across several real devices. More than two simultaneous editors in a shared draft: the OT transform is tested for convergence, but load on the per-draft row lock was not measured.
- **Load testing.** Query counts were measured on a near-empty database with caching off. Production timings need a production-like dataset.
- **Legal compliance.** GDPR (keystroke retention D-08, the public deletion log, no export), the UK Online Safety Act, NCMEC duties, AdSense policy: flagged as risks for a lawyer, not judged.
- **UX beyond heuristics.** There are no analytics or user research.
